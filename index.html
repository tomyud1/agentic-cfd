Agentic CFD

Reports written by an AI agent that solved each problem with OpenFOAM, given only the problem statement. Technion final project, supervised by Prof. Steven Frankel.

1. Plane Poiseuille flowPASS
Steady, linear, exact solution. Velocity profile within 0.0061 % of the exact solution.
2. Lid-driven cavity, Re = 100WARN
Steady, nonlinear, benchmark of Ghia et al. (1982). Velocity profiles within 0.009 U of the benchmark.
3. Stokes' first problemPASS
Transient, linear, exact solution. Velocity within 0.002 % of the plate speed.
4. Cylinder in a channel, Re = 100 (DFG 2D-2)WARN
Transient, nonlinear vortex shedding, FeatFlow benchmark. Drag, lift and Strouhal number inside their uncertainty bands; MPI scaling study.
5. Cylinder in a square duct, Re = 20 (DFG 3D-1Z)FAIL
Steady, 3D, up to 2.4 million cells, FeatFlow benchmark. Drag within 0.08 %; lift 1.7 % off, outside its band (sensitive to the mesh grading).
5b. GPU vs CPU study (RTX 4070, OGL plugin)PASS
Same answer on both sides. The GPU breaks even at about 2 million cells and is 1.13 times faster at 4.75 million; capped near 1.5 times by the CPU share of each iteration.
6. Heated pipe, Re = 200, uniform wall heat fluxWARN
Steady, first heat transfer, exact solution. Local Nusselt number within 0.005 % RMS; fully developed Nu 0.011 % off, just outside the agent's own 0.01 % rule.
