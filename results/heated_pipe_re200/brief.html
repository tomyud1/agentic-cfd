Laminar water flow in a tube heated with a uniform wall heat flux

Verdict of the case: WARN

Goal

The first case with heat transfer.

Solved with OpenFOAM. gpucfd cannot solve it yet: it has no temperature equation and no round pipe.

The problem

  • Water flows slowly through a small tube. After an unheated entrance, the wall is heated evenly.
  • Slow and steady (laminar flow).
  • Asked for: how the heat transfer develops along the tube, its final value, the length of the thermal entrance, and the wall and water temperatures.
  • Reference: the exact series solution of this problem.

Results

WARN. The simulated local Nusselt number matches the exact solution to \(0.005\,\%\) (RMS, finest mesh), the fully developed value to \(+0.011\,\%\) and the 5 % entrance length \(60.1\,D\) to \(-0.067\,\%\).
